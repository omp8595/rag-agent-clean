# Customer Support Knowledge Base

## Returns & Refunds

Our return policy allows customers to return items within 30 days of purchase for a full refund. Items must be in their original condition and packaging. To initiate a return, customers should log in to their account and navigate to Order History, then select the order and click "Request Return."

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item. The refund will be credited back to the original payment method.

## Shipping Information

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days and is free on orders over $50. Express shipping (2-3 business days) costs $12.99. Overnight shipping is available for $24.99.

All orders are shipped Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays. Once your order ships, you will receive a confirmation email with a tracking number.

## Account Management

To reset your password, click "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter your email address. You will receive a reset link within 5 minutes. If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

To update your billing or shipping address, go to Account Settings and click "Addresses." Changes take effect immediately for future orders but cannot be applied to orders already placed.

## Product Warranty

All products come with a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover damage caused by misuse, accidents, or normal wear and tear.

To file a warranty claim, contact our support team with your order number and a description of the issue. We may ask for photos of the defective product. Approved claims will result in a replacement or store credit.

## Subscription Plans

We offer three subscription tiers: Basic ($9.99/month), Pro ($19.99/month), and Enterprise (custom pricing). All plans include a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

To cancel your subscription, go to Account Settings, click "Subscription," and select "Cancel Plan." Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.
